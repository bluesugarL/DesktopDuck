# 💻 DeskDuck 🦆

An open-source desktop pet program deeply customized for the popular Bilibili streamer "Gugu Duck" from the game *Brown Dust 2*! It perfectly recreates Gugu Duck's adorable yet iconic image, featuring rich interactive gameplay. Let him reside on your desktop to accompany you while coding, watching videos, or gacha gaming!

---

## ✨ Features

### 👗 Outfit System

Built-in multiple exclusive high-quality skins (including the streamer's classic outfits and gaming-related meme costumes). Switch with one click to give DeskDuck a fresh look every day.

### 🍖 Feeding System
Interact directly on your desktop to feed him all sorts of quirky items. 
*Note: Feeding different items will trigger completely different dynamic animations and feedback!*

### ❤️ Affection System
Accumulate favorability through daily clicks, petting, and feeding. As your affection level rises, you will unlock hidden actions, exclusive voice lines/dialogues, and even special limited outfits!

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

### Method A: Ready-to-Run (For general users/fans)
1. Go to the **Releases** page of this repository.
2. Download the latest `DeskDuck_Vx.x.x.exe`.
3. Double-click the executable to bring your ducky to life!

### Method B: Local Development (For developers)
If you want to create new skins or add features for DeskDuck, you are more than welcome to Fork this repository!
